\subsection*{Point 5: IRDM Structural Organization}
\noindent\textbf{Editor's Comment:}
\textit{``Format of Research Article: IRDM. Please arrange your manuscript according to the below order: Abstract, Introduction, Results (subheadings compulsory), Discussion (no subheadings permitted, incorporate Limitations/Conclusions), Methods (subheadings compulsory), Declaration statements, References list, Figures, Tables and associated legends...''}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Response:}
The entire manuscript has been reorganized to strictly adhere to the IRDM structure:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Abstract:} Single paragraph, 179 words, unnumbered, no subheadings.
    \item \textbf{Introduction:} Unstructured, no subheadings.
    \item \textbf{Results:} Features compulsory thematic subheadings: (1) Problem Formulation and A Two-Level Taxonomy; (2) Financial Time-Series Extrapolation; (3) Financial Time-Series Imputation; (4) Financial Time-Series Synthesis; (5) Applications; (6) Evaluation Protocols; (7) Data Resources and Benchmarks.
    \item \textbf{Discussion:} Contains no subheadings (per editorial guidelines); organizes open challenges, limitations, and concluding outlook via bold paragraph lead-ins.
    \item \textbf{Methods:} Positioned directly after Discussion with compulsory subheadings: (1) Literature Retrieval Strategy; (2) Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria; (3) Data Extraction and Coding Protocol; (4) Review Dimensions and Taxonomy Synthesis.
    \item \textbf{Declarations:} Follows Methods and precedes References.
    \item \textbf{References:} Nature-compliant sequential numbered bibliography.

\noindent\textbf{Editor's Comment:}
\textit{``Please provide figure files individually in the system... Figure titles and legends must be removed from the image file... Add the legends in the main manuscript file after references. Present multi-panel figures on a single page, labeled a), b), c), etc...''}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Response:}
All requirements regarding figures, tables, and legends have been systematically addressed:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Repositioned after References:} Per the journal's IRDM layout instructions, all figure environments and tables have been moved from the main body and placed into dedicated sections after the References list (\texttt{Figure Legends and Figures} and \texttt{Tables}).
    \item \textbf{Figure Numbering and Callout Order:} All figure callouts in the text now appear in strict chronological order from Figure 1 through Figure 6.
    \item \textbf{Extended Legends:} Each figure caption begins with a concise bold summary sentence serving as the title (e.g., \textbf{Figure 1 | Evolution of Financial Time-Series Generation techniques over the past decade.}), followed by a comprehensive description (under 350 words) defining all subpanels (\textbf{a}, \textbf{b}, \textbf{c}), line styles, and color codes in plain English words without raw graphical symbols.
