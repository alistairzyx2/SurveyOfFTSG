\section{Conclusion} 
\label{sec:11}
This survey presents a systematic review of the financial time-series generation (FTSG) landscape. Our main contribution is a two-level taxonomy that unifies the field across both task-oriented and technique-driven dimensions, providing a structured framework for future studies.
Furthermore, we characterize the data resources by categorizing them into raw sources, curated databases, and carefully designed benchmarks. By outlining promising directions for addressing the unique challenges in this domain, we hope this work offers critical insights and advances the research community.
